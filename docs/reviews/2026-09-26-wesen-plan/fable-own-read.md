# Fable's own read (written before opening Astra's round 1)

Risks I see in my own plan, ranked:

1. **The step line praises the count.** "Es ist ein Stück näher gekommen!" after every tick makes the creature the reason to brush. Better: the creature reacts to the *act* ("Es hat gesehen, wie du geputzt hast") and the step is shown, not announced every day. Medium.
2. **Three shadows on day 1 is a collection hook.** Two silhouettes left "for later" invite "when do I get the others?" That's the catch-'em-all pull we said we'd leave out. Maybe keep the choice but let the other two go back into the forest, not sit waiting. Medium.
3. **Grow gate at 7 days after befriend is arbitrary.** For teeth, "allein" may already be true on day 1 for some kids. The honest gate is the parent's observation; the 7 days only stop a parent from rushing to please. Keep, but word it as "ab {Datum} fragen wir dich". Low.
4. **Move-out rewrites the routine.** Rebuilding today's quests mid-day could drop a done task and its flame; do it at a day change only (next morning's build), never mid-day. High for correctness.
5. **Merge rule `current` null vs non-null** is the trickiest case; keep a `moveOutId` so both devices agree. Medium.
6. **Attention away from Ronki** (A6): the creature should sit small, behind or beside, and Ronki does all the talking. The creature never speaks. Low.
7. **Test length:** befriend on day 5 and growth in week 2 is a fine shape. No compression. Bargaining usually shows in the first days after the novelty, so two weeks is enough.

Assumptions: A2 (parent honesty) is Marc himself, so fine for the test, weak for other families. A5 is real: Louis may ask where the task went; the goodbye scene should name it ("Zähne putzen kannst du jetzt allein, darum ist es nicht mehr im Nest").
