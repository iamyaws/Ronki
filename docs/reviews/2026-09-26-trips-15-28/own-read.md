# Own read before Astra: trips 15 to 28 (26 Sep 2026)

Written by Fable before opening any reviewer output.

## What

Fourteen new trips (t15 to t28) in `src/data/finchLines.de.json`, same shape as t01 to t14: place, emoji treasure, story (spoken when Ronki is back: at the evening start after a day trip, at breakfast after a dream trip), hook (the "Als Nächstes flieg ich ..." line the evening before). A daily child reaches trip 15 about two and a half weeks in; Louis started 26 Sep, so around mid-October. After trip 28 the order repeats honestly ("Da war ich schon mal").

Stories: 22 to 26 words (the first 14: 17 to 27). Places and emoji all unique across the 28.

## My rating of the weak spots

1. **t18 "mein eigenes Brüllen".** A dragon roar is funny, but Ronki is a gentle companion; roaring may not fit his character. Low to medium.
2. **t20 the cave, "ein bisschen dunkel".** Dark plus a cave could worry a sensitive six-year-old; the next sentence answers it with the fire the child taught him. I think it is fine, and the callback is the best line of the set.
3. **t28 a scale falls out.** "Keine Sorge, da wächst eine neue" should read like losing a milk tooth (first graders do), not like hurt. Worth a second eye.
4. **Seasons are mixed** (fog, apples, sunflowers, dandelions, puddles). The first 14 are mixed too; the fixed order reaches each child at a different time of year. Acceptable.
5. **Imitation:** jumping in puddles (fine), leaving a hedgehog alone (good), picking a Glockenblume (common, not protected), eating nothing wild. Fishing bark out of a Mühlbach: Ronki does it, not the child.
6. **Spoken German:** "hab's", "Ups", "Platsch", "Kapitän" should be fine for the Harry voice; the Whisper check will show.

Ship after Astra's pass, with at most small wording fixes. The voice batch is 28 files, about 2,800 characters.
