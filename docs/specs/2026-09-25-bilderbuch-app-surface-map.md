## Rules to hold while splitting the work

1. One primary action per view, and it is the loudest thing on its ground: cobalt pill on white or paper, sun pill on cobalt, sky or night. Never a cobalt or outlined pill on a blue ground.
2. No gradients in UI, no drop shadows, no blur. Depth is the ink outline, overlap, and at most the `.bb-lift` hard offset.
3. Sun and ember are never small text on white (`text-sun-deep` is the readable sun).
4. Grounds: white by default; sky, cobalt and night only for whole moments (breathing, calming, sleeping); paper only for book pages and cards.
5. Every Ronki comes from `MoodChibi`, `RonkiPortrait` or `RonkiArt`; no CSS dragons, no old `art/companion/*` or `art/dragon*` files.
6. Videos always through `SceneLoop` so reduced motion and Low Power Mode keep the poster.
7. Kid copy is first-grader German: short, concrete, imperative. No em-dashes anywhere.
8. Icons: `DoodleIcon`, not Material Symbols. When the last kid-facing Material glyph is gone, drop the Google link in `index.html`.
